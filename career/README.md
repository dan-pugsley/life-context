# Career

Professional life — work history, skills and career capital, trajectory, opportunities, and strategy.

Enables AI to act as a strategic career advisor: weighing decisions, managing optionality and downside risk (ABZ planning), and developing positioning and public presence. Stable data — history, skills, values, market position — lives in [profile.md](profile.md); volatile state — current role and terms, financial runway, active plans (ABZ), opportunity pipeline, public-presence experiments — lives in [status.md](status.md). New users start with [onboarding.md](onboarding.md).
