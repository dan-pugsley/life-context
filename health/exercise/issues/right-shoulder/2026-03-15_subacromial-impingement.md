## Condition

Right subacromial impingement — the rotator-cuff tendons get pinched under the acromion at the top of overhead movement. Onset ~March 2026, gradually, after ramping up overhead pressing volume on an online program with little upper-back work. Assessed by a physiotherapist (clinical exam, no imaging); consistent with impingement rather than a tear.

## Symptoms

- Pain at the top range of overhead pressing (barbell strict press worst)
- Ache on the outer/front shoulder with high-volume push-ups
- A "painful arc" raising the arm out to the side between ~70–120°
- No night pain, no weakness, no instability

## Prescribed rehab

1. Banded external rotation (elbow at side, 90°) — strengthens the cuff
2. Band pull-aparts and face pulls — upper-back / scapular retraction
3. Scapular retraction / setting drills — posture and control
4. Thoracic mobility (foam-roller extension, open-books) — restore extension lost to desk posture

Plus a graded return to pressing: landmine press → light DB overhead → barbell, progressing only while pain-free.

## Management

Symptom-light when the cuff/scapular rehab is done consistently and overhead load is progressed gradually. Flares if overhead volume jumps too fast or rehab lapses. Current restrictions and rehab stage are tracked in [status.md](../../status.md).

## Programming implications

- Build pulling and upper-back volume to balance pressing
- Reintroduce overhead pressing progressively, in pain-free range only (pain-monitoring gate)
- Cap push-up volume; include the band/cuff routine before upper-body days
- Otherwise no restriction on lower-body or pulling work
