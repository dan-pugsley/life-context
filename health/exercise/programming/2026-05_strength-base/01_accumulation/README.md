# 01 — Accumulation

## Overview

First block of the Strength Base macrocycle. Build a volume base and refine technique at moderate intensity (RPE 6–8) while integrating shoulder rehab. Three full-body-leaning sessions/week. The final week is a deload.

## Training split

| Day | Focus | Primary lift |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D1 | Lower (squat) | Back squat |
| D2 | Upper (shoulder-friendly push/pull) | Bench press |
| D3 | Full body + conditioning | Deadlift |

## Volume and intensity plan

| Week | Emphasis | Primary RPE | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | On-ramp | 6–7 | Establish working loads; leave 3–4 RIR |
| 2 | Build | 7 | Add reps/load via double progression |
| 3 | Peak volume | 7–8 | Highest sets of the block |
| 4 | Deload | 6 | ~55% volume, hold load |

## Exercise pool

- **Primary (consistent):** back squat, bench press, deadlift.
- **Accessories (may rotate):** Romanian deadlift, DB row, lat pulldown, split squat, landmine press, face pulls, DB curls, core (plank / carries).
- **Conditioning:** short intervals or loaded carries on D3 (10–15 min, moderate).
- **Rehab:** shoulder band/cuff routine before D2; thoracic mobility in all warm-ups.

## Progression model

Double progression: hit the top of the rep range at or under target RPE → add load next week (upper-body +2.5 kg, lower-body +5 kg).

## Deload (final week)

~55% volume (drop to 2 working sets), hold load, drop conditioning intervals, add extra mobility.

## Injury accommodations

Right shoulder (see [status.md](../../status.md)): no heavy barbell overhead; landmine/DB pressing in pain-free range only; cap push-up volume; rehab in before pressing days.

## Success criteria

Working loads established and progressing; sessions sitting at RPE 6–8 (not 9–10); no shoulder flare; DB overhead-press load advancing pain-free.
