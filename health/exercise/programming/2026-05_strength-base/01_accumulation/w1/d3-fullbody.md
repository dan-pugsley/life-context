# D3 — Full Body + Conditioning

## Warm-up

**Raise** (4 min): Bike or row, easy pace.

**Activate** (4 min):
- Glute bridges: 2×12
- Band pull-aparts: 2×15
- Bird dogs: 2×8 each side

**Mobilise** (3 min):
- Foam-roller thoracic extension: 2×8 (upper back only)
- Hip-hinge practice with a dowel: 2×8 (groove a flat back before loading)

**Potentiate**: Deadlift — ramp 1×5 at ~40 kg, 1×3 at ~60 kg.

## Primary

**Deadlift** — 3×6 @ RPE 6–7
- Start ~80 kg. Conservative on the on-ramp — technique over load.
- Rest: 2–3 min.
- Cue: bar over mid-foot, brace, push the floor away; flat back throughout. Reset each rep.

## Accessories

**Goblet squat** — 3×10 @ RPE 6–7
- ~16 kg DB/KB held at the chest. Rest: 60–90 s.

**Walking lunges** — 3×10 each leg
- Bodyweight to start. Rest: 60 s.

**Hanging knee raises** — 3×10
- Controlled, no swing. Rest: 45 s.

## Conditioning

**Intervals** (12 min): row or bike — 30 s hard / 90 s easy × 6 rounds. Moderate effort (~RPE 7 on the hard pieces), not a max test.

## Cool-down

- Walk 3–5 min easy
- Hamstring + hip-flexor stretch: 45 s each
