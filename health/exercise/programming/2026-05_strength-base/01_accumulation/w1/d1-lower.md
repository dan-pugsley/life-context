# D1 — Lower: Squat

## Warm-up

**Raise** (4 min): Bike or brisk incline walk, easy pace.

**Activate** (4 min):
- Glute bridges: 2×12
- Banded lateral walks: 2×10 each direction

**Mobilise** (3 min):
- Foam-roller thoracic extension: 2×8 slow reps (upper back only — never the lower back)
- Deep bodyweight squat hold + ankle rocks: 1 min
- Open-book rotations: 6 each side

**Potentiate**: Empty bar back squat 1×8, then ramp 1×5 at ~40 kg.

## Primary

**Back squat** — 3×8 @ RPE 6–7
- Start ~60 kg. If set 1 feels easier than RPE 6 (4+ in reserve), add 5 kg for set 2.
- Rest: 2–3 min between sets.
- Cue: brace before you descend, knees track over toes, controlled down and drive up. Full depth that you can keep an even back through.

## Accessories

**Romanian deadlift (RDL)** — 3×10 @ RPE 6–7
- ~50 kg to start. Hinge at the hips, soft knees, bar close to the legs, feel the hamstrings stretch; stop before the lower back rounds.
- Rest: 90 s.

**Bulgarian split squat** (rear foot elevated on a bench) — 3×8 each leg @ RPE 6–7
- Bodyweight or light DBs (~6 kg each) to start. Targets single-leg strength and stability.
- Rest: 60–90 s.

**Plank** — 3×40 s
- Straight line head to heels, ribs down, glutes tight.

## Cool-down

- Couch stretch (quad/hip flexor): 45 s each side
- Seated hamstring stretch: 45 s each side
