# D2 — Upper: Bench + Pull

## Warm-up

**Raise** (4 min): Row or bike, easy pace.

**Activate** (6 min) — shoulder band/cuff rehab (physio), straight sets:
- Banded external rotation: 3×12 each arm
- Band pull-aparts: 3×12
- Face pulls: 3×12
- Scapular retraction / setting: 3×10

**Mobilise** (2 min):
- Foam-roller thoracic extension: 2×8 (upper back only)
- Doorway pec stretch: 30 s each side

**Potentiate**: Empty bar bench 1×8, then 1×5 at ~30 kg.

## Primary

**Bench press** — 3×8 @ RPE 6–7
- Start ~42.5 kg. If set 1 is easier than RPE 6, add 2.5 kg.
- Rest: 2–3 min.
- Cue: shoulder blades set back and down, controlled descent to mid-chest, smooth press. Keep the shoulder stable — no pain.

## Accessories

**One-arm DB row** — 3×10 each arm @ RPE 6–7
- ~16 kg to start. Knee and hand on a bench, flat back, drive the elbow to the hip. Builds the upper-back volume the shoulder needs.
- Rest: 60–90 s.

**Landmine press** (rehab progression) — 3×10 each arm @ RPE 6, **pain-free only**
- Barbell in a corner/landmine; press up and slightly forward at an angle (easier on the shoulder than vertical). ~10 kg on the bar to start.
- This is the graded return to overhead — stop immediately if there's any pinch. Rest: 60 s.

**Lat pulldown** — 3×12 @ RPE 6–7
- ~35 kg. Full stretch at the top, drive elbows down, no excessive lean-back.
- Rest: 60 s.

**DB curls** — 2×12 @ RPE 7
- ~8 kg each. Rest: 45 s.

## Cool-down

- Cross-body shoulder stretch: 30 s each side
- Child's pose with side reach: 30 s each side
