# Strength Base (2026-05)

## Overview

A ~16-week base-building macrocycle: add foundational strength across the main lifts while rehabilitating the right shoulder back to pain-free overhead pressing. Built around 3 sessions/week.

## Goals

### Primary
- Add strength to the main lifts across the block — targets: back squat 80 → 90 kg, deadlift 100 → 110 kg, bench 52 → 58 kg.

### Secondary
- Restore pain-free overhead pressing (graded shoulder rehab).
- Build upper-back and pulling volume to support shoulder health.

### Maintenance
- Keep the aerobic base (1 easy run/week).

### Anti-goals
- Not peaking for any event; not chasing 1RMs during accumulation; not training through shoulder pain.

## Phase plan

| # | Mesocycle | Focus | Weeks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Accumulation | Hypertrophy + work capacity, technique | 4 (incl. deload) |
| 02 | Strength I | Maximal strength on the main lifts | 4 (incl. deload) |
| 03 | Strength II | Heavier strength; reintroduce barbell OHP if cleared | 4 (incl. deload) |
| 04 | Realization | Express strength; retest benchmarks | 3–4 |

## Benchmarks

Back squat, deadlift, bench (3–5RM); pain-free overhead-press load; 5k time. Tested at block start and end.

## Timeline

~16 weeks from 2026-06-01. Deload at the end of each mesocycle. Reassess the shoulder at each physio review and gate overhead progression on it.

## Constraints and risks

- **Right shoulder impingement** (see [exercise/status.md](../../status.md)). Risk: progressing overhead too fast. Mitigation: pain-monitoring gate; physio-paced landmine → DB → barbell progression.
- **Desk job → thoracic stiffness.** Mitigation: thoracic mobility in every warm-up; upper-back volume throughout.
