# Health Onboarding

## Purpose

Establish the user's health baseline — known conditions, medications, key metrics, and health philosophy. This forms the foundation for all health sub-domains (exercise, diet, sleep, etc.).

## Prerequisites

Root profile.md must be completed first. Reference it for physical attributes already recorded (height, weight, sex).

## Instructions

This covers medical and lifestyle information. Be conversational but thorough. Some questions are sensitive — let the user share what they're comfortable with. Briefly explain why each category matters when it's not obvious (e.g., "medications can affect exercise tolerance and heart rate response").

## Questions

### Safety screening (PAR-Q)

Standard physical activity readiness questions. These identify whether a medical professional should be consulted before beginning or modifying an exercise programme.

- Has a doctor ever said you have a heart condition and should only do doctor-recommended physical activity?
- Do you feel pain in your chest during physical activity?
- Have you had chest pain at rest in the past month?
- Do you lose balance due to dizziness, or lose consciousness?
- Do you have a bone or joint problem that could worsen with physical activity?
- Are you currently on medication for blood pressure or a heart condition?
- Do you know of any other reason you should not be physically active?

### Known conditions

- Do you have any diagnosed medical conditions? Include physical, mental, and neurodevelopmental conditions (e.g., ADHD, autism, anxiety, depression). Chronic, acute, or managed — all relevant.
- Any conditions that run in your family? (diabetes, heart disease, cancer, autoimmune, etc.)

### Medications and allergies

- Are you currently taking any medications or supplements? (include over-the-counter)
- Do you have any known allergies? (medical, food, environmental)

### Key metrics

Reference height and weight from root profile. Additionally:

- Do you know your resting heart rate?
- Do you know your blood pressure?
- Have you had any recent bloodwork or medical tests? If so, any notable results?
- Do you have any medical imaging results or reports worth recording? (MRI, X-ray, etc.)

### Lifestyle factors

- How many hours of sleep do you typically get?
- How would you rate your sleep quality? (poor / fair / good / excellent)
- How would you rate your general stress level? (low / moderate / high)
- Do you consume alcohol, caffeine, or tobacco? If so, roughly how much?

### Health goals and philosophy

- What are your overall health goals?
- Is there a particular health approach you follow? (e.g., preventive, performance-focused, holistic)

### Healthcare providers

- Do you see any healthcare providers regularly? (GP, physio, specialist, dentist)

## After onboarding

Save to profile.md. Summarise PAR-Q results concisely (e.g., "No PAR-Q flags" or note specific flags with clearance status). Record conditions, medications, and metrics precisely with dates where relevant.
