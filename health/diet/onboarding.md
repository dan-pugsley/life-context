# Diet Onboarding

## Purpose

Understand the user's current nutrition, dietary goals, restrictions, and relationship with food. Enables AI-guided meal planning and dietary adjustments.

Informed by clinical nutrition assessment frameworks used by registered dietitians, including 24-hour recall and food frequency methods.

## Prerequisites

Root and health profiles must be completed first. Reference them for physical attributes, conditions, allergies, and medications already recorded.

## Instructions

Nutrition is personal and sometimes sensitive. Be conversational and non-judgmental. Pay attention to the user's relationship with food — this affects how prescriptive recommendations should be. If the user already tracks macros or has a structured plan, focus on understanding what they have rather than asking basic questions.

## Questions

### Current eating pattern

- How many meals do you eat per day, and at roughly what times?
- Walk me through a typical day of eating — what do you have for each meal and snack?
- How much variety is there day to day, or do you eat largely the same things?
- Do you meal prep, cook daily, or eat out frequently?

### Dietary restrictions

- Do you follow any specific dietary approach? (e.g., vegetarian, keto, Mediterranean, flexible)
- Are there foods you avoid for medical, ethical, or personal reasons?
- Any food allergies or intolerances? (cross-reference with health profile)

### Preferences

- What foods do you love?
- What foods do you dislike or refuse to eat?
- How important is taste vs convenience vs nutrition to you?
- How much time are you willing to spend on food preparation?
- How skilled/confident are you in the kitchen?

### Nutrition goals

- What are you trying to achieve with your diet? (e.g., weight management, muscle gain, performance, general health, specific nutrient targets)
- Do you currently track macros, calories, or any other metrics?
- If so, what are your current targets and how consistently do you hit them?

### Supplements

- Do you take any supplements? (protein, vitamins, creatine, omega-3, etc.)
- What prompted you to start each one?

### Hydration

- How much water do you typically drink per day?
- What else do you drink regularly? (coffee, tea, juice, sports drinks, alcohol)

### Context

- What is your approximate weekly food budget?
- Do you eat with others regularly (family meals, social eating) or mostly solo?
- Is there anything about your current diet you're unhappy with or want to change?

## After onboarding

Save to profile.md structured under these headings:

- **Current pattern** — meals, timing, typical day
- **Restrictions** — medical, ethical, and preference-based
- **Preferences** — likes, dislikes, cooking habits, time constraints
- **Goals** — with specific targets if known
- **Supplements** — list with purposes
- **Hydration** — typical daily intake
- **Context** — budget, social eating, satisfaction with current diet
